Palefire

Palefire is the duo of Milan and Maika, musicians haunting Easton, PA. Songwriters in their own right, they summoned Palefire to honor, and resurrect, some of their favorite songs. More than 30 have been reborn so far with Palefire’s own haunting, ethereal, vampiric energy, among them Nancy Sinatra’s “Bang Bang,” The Cure’s “Lovesong,” Metric’s “Black Sheep” and Billy Idol’s “Eyes Without a Face.”

The pair are no strangers to a crowd. Between them they have played for thousands over their careers, are joined by hundreds of loyal fans every week, and have been heard tens of thousands of times online, with millions more views of their work beyond the stage. In short: people show up, and people listen.
